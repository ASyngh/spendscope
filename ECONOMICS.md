# ECONOMICS.md

SpendScope functions primarily as a lead-generation and qualification engine for Credex.

The product itself is free.

The business value comes from identifying startups that are overspending on AI infrastructure and routing high-intent leads toward discounted AI credits and optimisation opportunities.

---

# What Is a Converted Lead Worth?

Credex sells discounted AI infrastructure credits.

A realistic customer profile might look like:
- 10–50 person startup
- multiple AI subscriptions
- API usage
- recurring monthly infrastructure spend

Assume:
- average customer AI spend: $2000/month
- Credex captures or influences 20–30% of that spend
- effective monthly revenue to Credex: ~$300–$600/month
- average retention: 12 months

Estimated customer value:

```txt
$450/month × 12 months = $5400 LTV
```

Even with rough assumptions, a converted customer is likely worth several thousand dollars over time.

- medium traffic
- high relevance

$20–$80

Cost:
- $0

## 3. Referral / Sharing Loop

If audit URLs become shareable:
- founders compare stacks publicly

Estimated CAC:
```txt
Near zero
```

This is likely the highest-leverage long-term acquisition channel.

Example monthly funnel:

```txt
10,000 landing page visitors
↓
25% complete audit
↓
2,500 audits
↓
20% save/share report
↓
500 captured leads
↓
10% book Credex consultation
↓
50 consultations
↓
20% convert to paying customer
↓
10 customers
```

If each customer produces:
```txt
~$5400 LTV
```

Then:

```txt
10 × $5400 = $54,000 projected LTV generated
```

from one strong acquisition cycle.

# What Makes the Funnel Work

The critical factor is:
- immediate value before gating

SpendScope intentionally:
- does not require login
- shows savings instantly
- allows public sharing

This reduces friction significantly.

The lead capture happens:
- after value delivery
- after trust establishment
- after savings visibility

This likely improves conversion quality compared to traditional lead forms.

# Path to $1M ARR

Assume Credex wants SpendScope-driven revenue to reach:

```txt
$1M ARR
```

That equals:

```txt
~$83,000 MRR
```

Using estimated customer value:

```txt
$450 average monthly revenue/customer
```

Required active customers:

```txt
$83,000 / $450 ≈ 185 customers
```

Now assume:
- 2% audit-to-customer conversion
- 2500 audits/month

Expected customers/month:

```txt
2500 × 0.02 = 50 customers/month
```

Even accounting for:
- churn
- weaker conversion quality
- lower monetisation

the model becomes plausible if:
- the audit spreads organically
- founders trust the recommendations
- Credex closes high-savings accounts effectively

---

# Biggest Economic Risks

## 1. AI pricing changes rapidly

Audit logic must remain current.
Incorrect recommendations destroy trust quickly.

---

## 2. Low urgency users

Some startups may:
- acknowledge overspend
- still avoid switching due to workflow inertia

Savings alone may not create action.

---

## 3. Recommendation trust

If recommendations feel:
- generic
- exaggerated
- sales-driven

conversion rates collapse.

This is why SpendScope intentionally uses:
- conservative language
- deterministic audit math
- transparent reasoning
